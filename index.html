FAQ

What is the MOQ for HIMANN faucet orders?

For standard models the MOQ is 100 pieces per model; for OEM/ODM custom designs with logo printing it is 300 pieces per model. Sample orders of 1-5 pieces are accepted for quality evaluation.

How fast can HIMANN deliver OEM faucet orders?

Standard items ship in 7-15 days. OEM/ODM production typically takes 25-35 days after sample confirmation, depending on order volume.

What materials and quality tests does HIMANN use?

HIMANN uses brass gravity casting in its own foundry and runs 100% automated pressure testing on every unit, plus salt-spray and 500,000-cycle life testing in its 26,000 m² Quanzhou facility.

Does HIMANN provide technical drawings and volume quotations?

Yes. The dedicated export office replies with technical drawings and volume quotations within 12 hours of receiving an inquiry at sales@haimanfaucet.com.

Which certifications does HIMANN support for export markets?

HIMANN supports destination-market certification requirements such as CE, cUPC and WRAS on a per-project basis. Contact the export office at sales@haimanfaucet.com for the current certification list applicable to your market.
